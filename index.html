How Life After Rescue Works

A single unified pipeline connecting public reporters, field rescuers, vets, and donors[cite: 1].

01

Report & Auto-Match

Public snaps a photo with GPS location in 30 seconds[cite: 1]. The platform automatically routes the case to the nearest available verified NGO or rescuer[cite: 1].

02

Digital Medical Identity

Every animal gets a unique ID card with a QR code[cite: 1]. Vets log surgeries, daily medical updates, and vaccination progress directly to their profile[cite: 1].

03

Transparent Support & Adoption

Donors fund individual medical cases and receive direct photo updates[cite: 1]. Fully recovered animals seamlessly transfer to adoption listings[cite: 1].
